BHHU-ATRA Herpetology Data Pipeline

Bosnian Herpetological Association — Amphibia & Reptilia occurrence records

Reports updated weekly · All coordinates generalised to 10 km grid (public data policy)

This page provides access to automated data quality and coverage reports generated by the BHHU-ATRA occurrence database pipeline. The pipeline ingests field records collected via KoboToolbox and curated data maintained in Google Drive, transforms them into Darwin Core format, and validates them against 8 quality checks before publication to GBIF.

The database currently holds records for amphibians and reptiles of Bosnia and Herzegovina and neighbouring countries, compiled from field surveys, literature, and museum collections.

Pipeline Run

Latest sync metadata, record flow through pipeline stages, KoboToolbox records log, and output file checksums.
Data note: All occurrence coordinates in these reports are generalised to the nearest 10 km grid cell centre in accordance with the BHHU-ATRA open data policy. Exact localities are retained in the internal database and made available to verified researchers on request.
